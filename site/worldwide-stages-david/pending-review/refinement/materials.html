How David can make this reliable

  1. Mark the actual expansion parcel on the survey and establish its levels, soil and groundwater conditions.
  2. Agree the intended shell use, usable-area target and required utility capacity.
  3. Have the structure, drainage, waterproofing, access and building systems designed together.
  4. Select physical finish samples and the planting sizes; update the model to those choices.
  5. Reconcile model quantities with construction drawings and contractor pricing.

How closely would the finished site resemble the image?

The model can communicate the agreed form, proportions, finishes and planting layout. Actual stone varies, daylight changes, and trees take time to mature. Use matched model views, approved finish samples and planting schedules as the review baseline, then update them whenever the construction design changes.

There is no tenant equipment layout or verified power/cooling capacity in this shell study. Those decisions can materially change the service yards and interior.
